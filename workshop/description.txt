[h1]Tank[/h1]
A drivable main battle tank for ZED ZONE.

[list]
[*]Build a [b]Tank Kit[/b] at a workbench (60 Iron Plate, 20 Metal Component, 10 Copper Tube, 20 Iron Wire, 10 Copper Wire), carry it and press [b]Page Up[/b]: the tank is assembled in front of you and the kit is used up.
[*]Drives like the game's cars and [b]burns gasoline the same way[/b]. [b]Shift + Page Up[/b] next to it pours your gas cans in.
[*]The [b]turret follows your mouse[/b].
[*][b]Left click[/b]: main gun, fires 105 mm Tank Shells, a new ammo item (one per shot from the cargo or your backpack).
[*][b]Right click[/b]: roof machine gun, locks on to the zombie nearest your mouse (5.56mm or 7.62x39mm rounds).
[*]Heavy armour: the crew takes a fraction of the damage they would in a car.
[/list]

Settings and keys: Mod Menu (F11).
Coming next: crew seats for up to 4 players.
